# Acme Warranty Policy (v2.0)

1. Coverage
The manufacturer warranty period is stated on each product page and starts on the delivery date. Physical damage and liquid damage are not covered.

2. How to raise a warranty claim
Step 1: Open My Orders and select the item.
Step 2: Choose Raise Warranty Claim and describe the fault.
Step 3: Upload the invoice and a photo or video of the fault.
Step 4: An authorised service centre will contact you within 48 hours to arrange inspection or pickup.

3. Outcome
If the fault is covered, the item is repaired or replaced free of charge. If it cannot be repaired, a replacement or store credit is offered.
