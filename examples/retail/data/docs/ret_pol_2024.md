# Acme Retail: Return, Refund and Exchange Policy (v3.1)

1. Scope
This policy applies to all purchases made on the Acme online store by registered customers.

2. General conditions
Items must be returned with original packaging, tags and invoice. Returns are requested from the My Orders page.

--- page 2 ---
3. Return windows
The return window is counted in calendar days from the delivery date. A return requested on the last day of the window is accepted.

3.1 Electronics
Electronics may be returned within 10 days of delivery.

3.2 Fashion
Fashion items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

3.3 Home
Home and kitchen items may be returned within 15 days of delivery.

3.4 Non-returnable categories
Grocery items and beauty products are not returnable, for hygiene reasons.

--- page 3 ---
4. Fees
4.1 A restocking fee of 10% of the price paid applies to returns for change of mind.
4.2 A reverse shipping fee of Rs 60 applies to returns for change of mind.
4.3 No fees apply when the item is defective, damaged or the wrong item was delivered.

5. Refunds
5.1 Refunds are calculated on the net price actually paid after coupons and discounts.
5.2 Refunds above Rs 50,000 require approval by a customer-care supervisor before payment.
5.3 Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 working days of pickup.
